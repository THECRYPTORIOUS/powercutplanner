How we compared them

  • Price is the lowest we found from the brand's own UK store or a major UK retailer on 16 November 2026, and the card says where. Black Friday prices change daily, so always check today's price.
  • Price per kWh is the price divided by the battery size, so a bigger battery at a fair price ranks well.
  • Typical essentials means a fridge-freezer, Wi-Fi router, three LED lamps and two phone charges a day, about 1.14 kWh, with 85% of the rated capacity treated as usable. Work out your own home.
  • We haven't tested these units hands-on. Specs come from manufacturers and UK retailers. Pictures are our own illustrations, not product photos.
