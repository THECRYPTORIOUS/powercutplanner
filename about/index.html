About Power Cut Planner

Power Cut Planner is an independent UK website that helps households work out what backup power they actually need for a power cut, and what it would cost.

Most power station adverts quote big numbers without saying what they'd keep running. Our calculator starts from the other end: you tell it what you'd want running and for how long, and it shows which units can genuinely do that.

How we work

  • Same maths for every product. Run times come from the manufacturers' published capacity, with 15% allowed for conversion losses.
  • No hands-on testing claims. We say clearly when a figure is an estimate, and we don't pretend to have tested things we haven't.
  • Calm, practical advice. We link to the government's own power cut advice rather than scary headlines.
