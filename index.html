      Before a power cut

        Common questions

        Why do I need more than my appliances add up to?

        A power station stores energy as DC and converts it to 230 V mains, which wastes some of it. Some energy also goes on keeping the unit itself switched on. We allow 15% for this, so you're not caught short.

        Will a power station run my gas boiler?

        Often, yes. A gas boiler's controls and pump draw roughly 100 W. But most boilers are wired into a fused spur rather than a plug, so an electrician needs to fit a proper plug-in connection first. Never wire a power station into your home's circuits yourself.

        Can I recharge it with a solar panel during a long power cut?

        Yes, but go easy on the maths in winter. A UK solar panel produces only a small share of its rated watts on a grey December day, so treat solar as a top-up rather than a replacement for battery size.

        Is a petrol generator a better idea?

        It's cheaper per kWh, but it's noisy and needs fuel stored safely. Above all, a petrol generator must never be run indoors or in a garage because of carbon monoxide. A battery power station is silent and safe to use inside.
